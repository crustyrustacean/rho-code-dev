Meet **rho**

Your local AI coding agent. Built for developers who want intelligent assistance that actually understands their codebase.

<section class="feature-section">

## **Understands Your Code**

rho reads your files, runs your tests, and fixes your errors. It doesn't just generate code—it understands your project structure and can navigate complex codebases with context.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Rust-First Experience**

Built specifically for Rust development with deep integration for `cargo`, compiler diagnostics, and the Rust ecosystem. But flexible enough to work with any language.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Runs Locally**

No cloud dependencies. Your code stays on your machine. rho runs entirely locally and respects your privacy while providing powerful AI assistance.

</section>

---

### **Key Features**

- **File Operations**: Read, write, and edit files with precision
- **Compiler Integration**: Parse `cargo check` output and apply fixes
- **Test-Driven Development**: Run tests and diagnose failures
- **Codebase Navigation**: Understand project structure and dependencies
- **Shell Integration**: Execute commands and interpret results
- **Privacy-First**: Entirely local operation
